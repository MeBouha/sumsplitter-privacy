This Privacy Policy describes how SumSplitter ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information. The App is developed and published by Bouha ("the Developer").

SumSplitter does not collect any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, or any other identifying information.

All game data — including your current level, hearts, undo count, and saved progress — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

4. Children's Privacy

SumSplitter does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since the App does not collect any personal data at all, it is safe for users of all ages.

5. Data Security

Because we do not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no risk of your personal information being exposed through the App. All game progress is stored locally on your device and is protected by your device's own security measures.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is nothing for us to delete, modify, or export on your behalf. To remove all game data, simply uninstall the App from your device.
